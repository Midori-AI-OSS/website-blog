## the images took forever

I think the images may have taken almost as much brain power as some of the writing did.

there are a lot of little things in them that I really wanted right.

one of the stupidest sounding ones was Leo's left.

like literally just making sure Leo's left was actually Leo's left.

this sounds easy until you are working with generated images and somebody is facing the camera or turned a little bit and suddenly left means four different things depending on who is talking about the image.

I spent way too long on that.

and dont even get me started on Luna sitting on Leo's lap.

they are twins.

I wanted the image to very obviously show that they are twins being close and comfortable with each other and nothing else.

that became a whole fucking thing.

where Luna was sitting.

how Leo was sitting.

their faces.

their body language.

how close they were.

what the image actually looked like when you stopped thinking about the prompt and just looked at the thing.

I wanted that one 110% safe.

I would rather be way too careful with something like that than put out an image where people look at it and go what the fuck is happening here.

I did get it fixed.

and I am really happy with the final one.

there was also the classroom stuff, library stuff, tunnel stuff, mirror stuff, the circuit image, glasses, Luna's letter, the plane thing, and a bunch of other little images where I would get one part right and another part would be wrong.

so then do it again.

and again.

then wow cool, somebody moved to the wrong side.

do it again.

the tunnel alone had me changing how I described the camera because it kept trying to show way more hallway than I wanted.

so yea.

## github auth is still being a little shit

something else that has bugged the shit out of me is the github auth problem I talked about in the last post.

I keep trying to fix this thing and it keeps finding a new way to show back up.

the first fix was basically okay, we are checking this way too much.

I have 20+ envs.

they do not all need to keep asking github the same fucking question.

so I made the answer stick around longer instead of checking over and over.

that looked good.

then it happened again.

cool.

so I reopened the issue.

the next fix went a lot deeper.

instead of a bunch of different parts of runner all deciding they need to check github right now, they can share one check.

if a bunch of stuff asks at once, they wait for the same answer instead of all running `gh auth status`.

that version had some race stuff found in review too.

because apparently even the fix for too many auth checks needed an auth check race.

one problem was if github had a little temp failure.

I did not want that bad answer sitting around for five mins telling everything I was logged out.

another was if the login changed while a check was already running.

the old check could come back later and put the old info back.

both of those got worked on too.

I am still not calling this fixed.

nope.

I learned my lesson last time.

it can behave for a while first.

then maybe I will trust it.

maybe.
