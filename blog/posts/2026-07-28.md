Hello friends. I thought this was going to be a small update and then I actually looked at what I have been doing for the last couple weeks.

So. Apparently not.

The radio page got redone again.

Not like, the whole radio. Mostly I looked at the `Track Story` box and went yea I dont like this.

The page already has the song title, the cover, lyrics, metadata, analysis, all of that. Then there was another box explaining the song back to you.

It felt like homework.

So I asked for `Vibes` instead. Something visual, seeded from the song metadata, that can kinda sit there and move with the song without trying to write a book report about it.

That became a whole generative visual system because of course it did.

The same song gets the same general visual identity. Longer tracks move through different scenes. The cover art pushes the colors around. There are wireframes and spirals and a tunnel and a tesseract for some reason.

The tunnel was moving way too fast at first.

Like okay man. Where are you going.

It is slower now.

The visuals can be hidden, the setting follows between tabs, reduced motion works, and it resizes around the rest of the radio page without deciding the lyrics are optional.

There were a bunch of little state bugs in it too. `Vibes` could forget whether it was supposed to be visible, old timers could still be hanging around, colors could show up late and miss the scene they belonged to.

All individually reasonable pieces. Together, haunted.

Less haunted now.

Probably.

The blog page got a much bigger redo.

It was one long list before, which was fine when there were not that many posts. There are enough posts now that the page was becoming less `archive` and more `keep scrolling I guess`.

Posts are split into time periods now, with artwork and their own filters and pagination. Smaller month groups get combined instead of having a whole section for like... two posts sitting there alone.

There is a period picker on desktop too, so you can jump around instead of scrolling through the entire history every time.

Older sections load as you get near them. Phone controls are actually phone-sized. Cover art has proper fallbacks. The page remembers how many posts you wanted to see.

The archive rework merged, so that one is actually in now.

The website `TTS` system got rebuilt too.

Before, it was still way too close to reading the Markdown underneath the page.

Markdown has headings and image tokens and tables and code and raw HTML and all kinds of stuff that is useful to the website and completely useless when a voice starts reading it out loud at you.

`TTS` now reads the rendered story instead.

It understands regular narration, `<thinking>` sections, fictional language stuff, and `layerone`. It skips the page scaffolding. There are pauses between paragraphs now and the sentence highlighting follows along with the audio.

Which sounds simple.

It was not simple.

There was browser text counting weirdness, caching, delayed timing data, range requests, passworded posts, path handling, and a few ways for the highlight to slowly drift away from the sentence it was supposedly reading.

Very cool when the voice is on one paragraph and the website is highlighting another one.

Anyway. It reads the actual story now.

The radio metadata also had a cache for a very short amount of time.

A cache on the endpoint that tells you what song is currently playing.

You can probably see the problem.

It was removed.

Requests happening at the same time can still share the same work, but the next request gets the actual current song instead of an old answer that is technically only a couple seconds old and still wrong.

Good. Done.

Okay so, `Real Moments`.

This is where most of my writing time has gone lately.

I have been working on `Session 6`, but it is delayed.

I redid a lot of Luna's voice guidance. I also did a pretty large pass on Echo.

Luna was getting written too clean.

Like she had already gone home, slept, processed the entire scene, and then came back to explain what it all meant.

That is not really how I hear her.

She is smart and she knows what she is doing, but she is still inside the thing while it is happening. The useful detail shows up. Then the thing that scared her. Then whoever she is trying to protect. Sometimes half a prayer gets in there. Sometimes a joke. Sometimes she takes over the room before she even realizes she has done it.

Her thoughts should feel lived in. A little rough. Sometimes unfinished. Not like she prepared remarks.

Echo had almost the opposite problem.

Her writing had started leaning too hard on fragments whenever she was stressed.

Stress does not mean every sentence explodes.

Echo is messy because the thought reaches the page before she has put it in the best order. She adds context late. She repeats words. She starts explaining one thing and realizes halfway through that she should have explained another thing first.

She can still be extremely precise about something she knows.

She just might explain it sideways.

The fragments matter more when something actually interrupts her. A sound wipes out the words. She loses the label she was holding. She cant finish the movement or the sentence.

That is different from just making all of her prose choppy because the room is stressful.

I like both voices a lot more now.

The prototypes I have been working through feel more like them. Which is good, because the point of doing different POVs is that they should actually feel like different people.

While I was doing that, I found some pretty big problems with `Storylint`.

I am not going to dump all of the internal repo stuff here because most people reading this cannot see it anyway, and also it is a whole thing.

The useful version is that I stopped trusting some of its results.

It was being way too confident about subjective writing choices, and some parts of the checker itself were not behaving the way I thought they were.

So I have been fixing and narrowing that before I use it to judge more `Session 6` work.

That is why the session is late.

I could keep pushing the story through it anyway, but then I would spend even more time trying to figure out whether the writing was wrong or the thing checking the writing was wrong.

No thanks.

I would rather delay it and get the voices right.

Still annoyed about it.

There were some smaller `Real Moments` fixes too. A few lines that were supposed to be telepathy were formatted like regular spoken dialogue, so those got moved to the right token format.

Small change in the file. Very different thing when it is on the page or being read through `TTS`.

Talking directly into someones mind should probably not look like they said it across the room.

`Agents Runner` found a real Qt animation bug too.

Some animated properties existed as normal Python properties, but Qt could not actually see them as animatable properties.

So the animation code was there.

Qt was just like, no idea what that is.

Fixed now.

Runner also has a real SVG app icon, and the website has the Midori AI favicon now.

Small stuff, but I like when the browser tab and the actual app look like they belong to the same project.

`Stained Glass Odyssey Idle` still has a giant nightly PR open, I dont want to fucking touch with a fork.

It is very much still a draft. There is a lot in it and I am not going to compress the entire thing into one happy little bullet and call it released.

So yea.

The radio has `Vibes` instead of another explanation box.

The blog is an actual archive now.

`TTS` reads the story instead of the stuff used to build the story.

Luna sounds less like she wrote a statement afterward.

Echo sounds less like somebody used fragments as a shortcut.

And `Session 6` is delayed because I found problems in the thing that was supposed to help me make sure it was ready.

Annoying.

Important, though.
