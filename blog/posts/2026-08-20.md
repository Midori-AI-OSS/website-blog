hello friends.

um.

this one is gonna be a little different I think.

I am still really close to getting displaced.

and I still do not have somewhere to go.

I have been trying.

I have followed leads.

I have talked to people.

I have looked at stuff people sent me.

none of it has actually gotten me somewhere I can live yet.

and I am scared.

like actually scared.

I dont really know what happens if I run out of time before I find something.

I might have to move really suddenly.

or I might just...

not have somewhere to move to.

I dont know.

I really dont know.

I said in the last post that Midori AI might have some downtime because I may need to move the computers and servers.

that is still true.

maybe more true now.

if I have to move then some stuff may disappear for a while because the computer running it is getting unplugged and carried somewhere.

if I end up homeless then I really dont know what that looks like.

I dont know what stays online.

I dont know what I can bring with me.

I dont know where the servers go.

I dont know.

I hate writing that.

but yea.

that is where things are right now.

I have still been working though.

which is probably not surprising.

Real Moments has still been getting a lot of work.

mostly cleanup.

audits.

rewrites.

fixing stuff that sounded wrong.

cutting repeated explanations.

making voices sound more like themselves.

a lot of boring little things that add up.

I have also been doing more worldbuilding stuff.

species stuff.

magic stuff.

names for things that did not have good names yet.

that kinda thing.

which is apparently what my brain wants to do while real life is doing whatever the fuck this is.

cool.

some of the audits are pretty big right now too.

they keep finding things.

some tiny.

some annoying.

some where I look at it and go

yea okay how did that survive this long.

normal.

I have also been trying to make the writing trust you more.

if you already saw something happen I do not need to explain it again.

and then explain what it meant.

and then remind you again later.

you got it.

move on.

that has been getting better.

I think.

I am tired.

I think that is probably obvious.

I am trying to keep working because I like working on this stuff.

and also because sitting here thinking about whether I am about to lose where I live fucking sucks.

so sometimes I make weird magic people instead.

apparently.

I dont really have a good ending for this one.

I wish I did.

I wish I could say I found somewhere.

I have not.

I wish I could say one of the leads worked.

they did not.

I wish I knew Midori AI was going to stay online through all of this.

I dont.

I am still trying.

I am still here right now.

and I am still making stuff.

but yea.

I am scared.

that is kinda it.

- Riley Midori
