hello friends.

okay.

housing first because I have an actual answer now.

I found a place.

I move in on the 9th.

which is a very different sentence from the ones I have been writing lately.

for a while everything was just

maybe.

maybe this place works.

maybe that place works.

maybe I have somewhere to go.

and I hated that answer.

because maybe is not a very comfortable place to live.

but now I have a move-in date.

which is much better.

still a little scary.

because moving is always moving.

but this is the good kind of scary.

the kind where there are boxes and figuring out where things go.

not the kind where I am wondering if I have somewhere to put the boxes.

so.

that is nice.

also.

I apparently have a very normal amount of stuff to move.

by normal I mean:

9 servers.

6 computers.

4 UPS units.

3 AC units.

and a bunch of cables.

this is fine.

this is a completely normal amount of computer for one person.

probably.

anyway.

Midori AI is also in a much better place than it was before.

friends helping host things has made a huge difference.

the website.

the blog.

the bots.

stuff can keep running even if my physical location changes.

that is a much nicer problem to have.

The Archive Moments shelf also landed on the blog.

which gives old lore and archive stuff somewhere better to live.

I like this kind of work.

not everything needs to be a huge new feature.

sometimes things just need a place.

Real Moments has still been eating my brain.

again.

more audits.

more finding things.

more looking at old writing and going

why did I explain that three times.

please stop.

you already saw it.

move on.

trust the reader.

especially with TTS.

because hearing the same idea three times is worse than reading it three times.

anyway.

I move on the 9th.

that is still a weird thing to type.

A few weeks ago I was writing about not knowing where I was going.

now I am figuring out where the servers go.

which is a much better problem.

there is still a lot to do.

boxes.

computers.

probably some cables that have escaped and started their own civilization.

but.

I have a place.

and that is a really good thing.

- Riley Midori
