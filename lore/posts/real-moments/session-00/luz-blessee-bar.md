Luna looked at me.

"I think it was the ice."

Her face changed immediately.

"Wait."

She sat straighter.

"Marisol said something when she saw us. Noc..."

Leo looked at her.

Luna frowned at the table.

"Noct... fuck. It was something like that."

I tapped the card once.

"Call them?"

Luna pulled out her phone.

"Yea."

She called the number on the card and put it on speaker.

It rang twice.

"Stillglass House."

Luna glanced around the table.

"Hi. Marisol gave us one of your cards last night. There were five of us at Eureka Live. She told us to rest and then contact you."

There was a short pause and some typing.

"One moment."

We waited.

I looked at the card again.

Stillglass House was a name on a card and Marisol pointing us toward it. That was about all I had.

The person came back.

"Yes. I have her note. Five guests from Eureka Live. She requested that you come in together once everyone was rested."

Luna looked at me, then Leo.

"Okay. She also said a word when she first saw us. I can only remember the beginning. Noct... something?"

"Noctilux."

Luna pointed at the phone.

"Yes. That."

"That's in the note. Noctilux is a crystalline contaminant we work with here. Marisol marked suspected exposure. The reader can go through the rest with all five of you in person."

I had my phone out before I really thought about doing it.

I typed Noctilux.

Just that.

The first results filled in while Luna kept talking.

"What should we do tonight?" she asked.

"Keep resting. Eat, hydrate, keep things easy. If everyone continues improving, come in tomorrow morning. Bring the card."

"Okay."

I opened one of the results.

There was a photo at the top.

Clear pieces. Uneven little chunks. Translucent edges.

I stared at it.

Under the image, the short description started with clear crystalline material and then, a few words later, said commonly mistaken for clear drink ice.

"Oh my god."

Everybody looked at me.

I turned the phone around.

"It was the ice. It was absolutely the fucking ice."

Echo leaned toward the screen.

Leo got up enough from his chair to see it better.

Luna was still holding her phone near the middle of the table.

The person from Stillglass said, "Is everything all right?"

Luna looked at my screen.

"Yea. We just found the public entry."

"Understood. Bring any details you remember about what was served and when symptoms started. The reader will take it from there."

"We will. Thank you."

"You're welcome. We'll expect you tomorrow morning."

Luna ended the call.

I kept looking at the picture for another second.

It looked like ice.

Of course it looked like ice. That was the whole fucking problem.

I set my phone down.

"Okay. So. Last night at Main, before I passed out against a wall like a genius, I was talking to Weave about the drinks."

The orb tightened a little over the table.

"They were all different. Leo had water. The drinks themselves made zero sense as the shared thing. Then I realized every glass had ice."

I looked at Weave.

"That's where we landed."

__Yes. We reached the same conclusion at Main Stage. The ice was the strongest common factor.__

Luna sat back slowly.

"Jesus Christ."

"Yea."

Echo looked from the phone to me. "So you knew last night?"

"I suspected it last night. Now I'm looking at the thing and it literally says it gets mistaken for drink ice, so... yea. I feel pretty fucking good about that answer."

Leo looked at the Stillglass card.

"That explains why Marisol sent us there."

"Yea."

That part fit now.

Which left the blood thing.

I took a drink and stared at the table for a second.

I could have let it wait until Stillglass.

I really wanted to.

Echo was already watching me.

Fuck.

"There's another thing."

The table stayed quiet.

That helped.

"Before everybody started getting really messed up, I kept seeing the whole room bleeding."

Luna's expression tightened.

I kept going.

"Everybody. You guys. The bartender. People on the floor. Everybody was looking at me and there was blood everywhere."

I stopped for a second.

Saying it out loud brought the image back way too cleanly.

"I was fine. I looked down and I was clean. Then it would be gone."

Echo moved a little closer in her chair.

Leo asked, "When did it start?"

"When the first ice hit my glass."

The table went quiet.

I rubbed my thumb along the edge of the card.

"That's the part I still can't do anything with. The ice fits the rest. That image... I don't know. And the stupid part is the blood isn't even the thing I keep getting stuck on."

Echo looked at me. "What is?"

"Everybody looking at me."

There it was.

I hated how much better it felt to finally say that part.

"Like every single person had turned toward me. I don't know why. I don't know what that means. I don't know if it means anything. That's just the part that keeps coming back."

Echo reached over and put her hand on my arm.

I left mine where it was under hers.

Luna said, "We tell Stillglass that too."

"Yea."

Leo nodded once. "All of it."

Luna looked up at Weave. "Did Riley tell you about that last night?"

__Yes. Riley told me at Main Stage.__

"And?"

__We did not have enough information to explain it.__

"Yea," I said. "That's the shitty part."

We sat with it for a while.

Then, because apparently life had decided we were allowed to have more than one kind of problem at a time, Luna's laptop made the email sound again.

She stared at it.

I looked at her.

"Seven more questions?"

"If there are seven more questions, I'm deleting the internet."

"Do it."

Leo said, "Please don't."

Echo smiled.

The room loosened a little.

We went back to our own stuff for another hour.

I fixed one thing in the model and broke something else. Luna answered her emails. Leo finished whatever he was reading. Echo eventually abandoned the cereal and moved back to the couch.

Some of Weave stayed with her there. The rest drifted between the kitchen and hall, sometimes crossing my view above the laptop before spreading out again.

By evening I was tired in a normal way.

That felt almost luxurious.

Leo heated up more food. I ate chicken strips and fries again because they were there and I liked them and that was enough of a reason. Luna talked more than she had earlier. Echo looked steadier when she crossed the room. Everybody let those things be ordinary.

When it got late, I took my bag back to the spare room.

Echo was already setting herself up on the couch.

"You good out here?" I asked.

"Yea."

A loose cluster of Weave drifted down near the back of the couch.

Echo looked up. "You're staying out here?"

__Part of me will remain nearby.__

"Okay."

She looked toward the hall. "Are you going to sleep?"

"That's the idea."

She kept looking at me.

"I will try very hard to perform sleep correctly."

"Okay."

That was apparently acceptable.

I went into the spare room and left the door cracked.

A few loose particles of Weave passed the doorway while another cluster stayed with Echo in the living room.

__Good night, Riley.__

"Night, Weave."

I put my glasses on the nightstand beside my drink and lay down.

The blood image still showed up when I closed my eyes.

I kept getting stuck on all of them looking at me.

Now I had a name for the ice.

Noctilux.

That was one thing I could put somewhere.

The rest could wait until morning.

Eventually I slept.

I woke up hungry.

That was honestly a pretty good sign.

I put my glasses on, grabbed my drink, and went to the kitchen.

Leo had eggs going for himself and Luna. Echo was at the table with dry cereal again. I opened the fridge, found the leftover chicken strips and fries, and reheated them.

Luna looked at my plate when I sat down.

"Morning food."

"Food is food."

"I know."

I took another bite.

"Good."

She smiled into her drink.

Weave floated over the middle of the table in her orb while we ate.

The Stillglass card was still there from the night before.

It stayed there until we had all eaten something.

Then Leo looked around the table.

"Everybody still up for going?"

Echo nodded.

Luna said, "Yea."

__Yes. I am ready.__

I picked up the card and slid it back into my bag.

"Yea. Let's go find out what the fuck happened to us."

We cleaned up enough to make coming home later easier.

At the door, Luna and Leo put their shoes back on. Echo got hers on and stood carefully. I stepped into my flip-flops and adjusted my bag across me.

Weave flowed toward the door ahead of us.

A few minutes later, all five of us were leaving together for Stillglass.
