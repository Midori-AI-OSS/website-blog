I wanted to keep these earlier versions of Real Moments lore. They changed, they grew, and sometimes they were rebuilt into something new, but they still matter to me.

The story names and story order here match the versions that once appeared in Real Moments, so you can follow the same moments back through time and see how the characters, scenes, and world grew into their newer forms.

These are not the current versions of the lore. They are here because the path matters too.

Read at your own pace. Compare gently. I did not want any of it erased.
